# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period may request remote work, subject to their role and manager approval.

## Remote Work Schedule

Eligible employees may work remotely up to **2 days per week**.

Employees are expected to remain available during normal working hours while working remotely.

## Approval Process

Remote work requests must be submitted through the HR system at least one working day in advance.

Managers may approve or reject requests based on operational requirements.

## Responsibilities

Employees working remotely are responsible for maintaining a suitable working environment and a reliable internet connection.

Employees must attend scheduled meetings and remain reachable through the company's communication channels.

## Exceptions

Certain roles that require physical presence at company facilities may not be eligible for remote work.

# Annual Leave Policy

## Annual Leave

Employees are entitled to **20 days** of paid annual leave each calendar year.

Unused annual leave can be carried over for up to one year with manager approval.

## Sick Leave

Employees may take paid sick leave when supported by a medical report.

## Personal Leave

Employees may take up to **5 days** of paid personal leave each year.

## Approval Process

All leave requests must be submitted through the HR system and approved by the employee's manager before the leave starts.

## Emergency Leave

Emergency leave requests can be submitted after the leave begins if the employee cannot notify the manager beforehand.

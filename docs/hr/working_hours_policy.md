# Working Hours Policy

## Standard Working Hours

The standard working schedule is **40 hours per week**, normally distributed across five working days.

The standard working hours are from **09:00 to 18:00**, including a one-hour lunch break.

## Attendance

Employees are expected to be available during core working hours and to attend scheduled meetings and work-related activities.

## Flexible Working

Employees may request flexible working arrangements with manager approval.

Flexible working arrangements must not negatively affect team collaboration or business operations.

## Overtime

Work exceeding the standard weekly working hours may be considered overtime.

Overtime must be approved by the employee's manager before it is performed, except in documented emergency situations.
